Privacy Policy

At Andrew CyberSec Learning Platform, your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect
  • Name, email, and contact information when you register or contact us.
  • Usage data: which pages you visit, videos you watch, and interactions with our content.
  • Cookies to enhance your browsing experience.
2. How We Use Your Information
  • To provide and improve our learning platform.
  • To respond to your messages and inquiries.
  • To send updates about courses, new content, or important announcements (you can opt out anytime).
3. Sharing Your Information
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We may share information with service providers that help us operate the platform, but only as necessary.
4. Your Rights
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information.
  • You can opt-out of marketing emails at any time.
  • We respect your privacy and handle your data responsibly.
